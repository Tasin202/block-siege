## 🎮 About

Zombies are marching on your tower. Build your defenses, survive wave after wave, loot gems and materials, and upgrade your army between rounds. Every upgrade is permanent, so every run makes you stronger.

## ✨ Features

- 🧱 **Full 3D blocky world** with real lighting and shadows
- 🔫 **4 defenders:** Blaster, Miner, Wall and Cannon, each with 8 upgrade levels
- 🧟 **5 enemy types:** walkers, runners, tanks, 🏹 skeleton archers that shoot from far away, and 🐉 a dragon boss every 5 waves
- ⛏️ **Safe mining rows:** miners go in the dirt rows, where zombies never attack
- 💎 **Loot and upgrades:** earn gems and materials every wave and spend them in the Workshop
- 🎵 **Original music:** calm tunes between waves, a fast beat during fights
- 🔭 **Pan and zoom** to get the perfect view
- ⚙️ **Settings:** difficulty, game speed, music, effects, fullscreen
- 💾 **Auto-save** on your device

## 🕹️ How to play

1. Pick a defender from the side bar, then tap a tile to place it.
2. Put ⛏️ **Miners** in the dirt rows for scrap ⚡, and fighters on the grass lanes.
3. Protect your 🏰 tower. If its health hits zero, the run is over.
4. After each wave, spend loot in the 🔧 **Workshop** to level up.

**Controls:** tap/click to place · drag to scroll · pinch or scroll wheel to zoom · `1-5` to select · `Esc` to pause

## 📱 Install on Android

1. Open the game link in Chrome.
2. Tap **⋮ → Add to Home screen** (or download the APK from [Releases](../../releases)).
3. Play full screen, even offline after the first load.

> Best played in **landscape**.
